## The pitch

> We rent trained ravens to hand-deliver breakup letters to exes who ghosted you, because jilted romantics happily pay $40 for a dramatic goodbye their ex can neither ignore nor delete.

## Why it fits the brief

The idea is genuinely bizarre: a bird courier for breakups is not a familiar business with a strange adjective bolted on. Yet the business logic survives a second look — it names the odd thing being sold (trained ravens hand-delivering breakup letters), who pays (jilted romantics, $40 a letter), and why it holds together (a dramatic goodbye that can't be ignored or deleted is exactly the closure people will pay for). One sentence, 183 characters, with a turn from laughing to nodding: the raven is the joke, the paying customer is the punchline that makes it real.

## How to run

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=output python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (pure Python standard library) reads `entry.txt` beside it, validates it is 1–200 characters, and writes its exact bytes to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
